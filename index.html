How to play

Enter each multiplication answer on the keypad before the timer runs out.

A round finishes when every active square is green.

Journey

Follow 36 stages through the tables. Start with 10 seconds per question, then try the 5- and 3-second levels. Replay a cleared stage to beat your time.

Manual

Pick one table from 2 to 12, or choose All to mix them together. Set the timer to 10, 5 or 3 seconds per question.

Reading the grid

  • Green Cleared or inactive
  • Orange One correct answer left
  • Red Extra practice
