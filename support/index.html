Common questions

For a bug report, include your device model, system and app versions, and what happened.

GreenSquare runs on iOS and iPadOS 17 or later. Play in portrait on iPhone or in either orientation on iPad.

Why does the game show the answer?

GreenSquare shows the answer when time runs out or you enter an incorrect number. Type the number shown, then tap the return arrow or press Return or Enter on an iPad keyboard. You’re more likely to see that fact again during the round.

What do the grid colours mean?

Green means you’ve cleared the fact or it falls outside your chosen table. Orange needs one correct answer before time runs out. Red needs extra practice. Clear every square to finish the round.

How do I change a level reward?

On the setup screen, tap the gear to open Settings. Under Rewards, the child and a parent, guardian or teacher can agree on a real-world reward for each of the three Journey levels. Edit the rewards, tick the agreement and tap Done. You arrange the rewards outside the app.

How do I change sound or visual effects?

Open Settings using the gear on the setup screen. Switch Background Music and Sound Effects on or off separately, or turn on Reduce Visual Effects to reduce motion and flicker. During a round, tap the speaker button to mute or unmute the music.

How do I remove an adventurer?

Tap Log Out on the setup screen. Tap the bin beside the adventurer’s name, type delete and tap Delete.
