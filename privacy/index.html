Privacy policy

GreenSquare works offline. Adventurer profiles, settings and progress are saved on the device.

Updated 19 November 2026

On this page

  • Data collection and sharing
  • Removing an adventurer

What is saved

GreenSquare keeps separate records for each adventurer so they can return to their settings and pick up their Journey.

  • Profile and rewards include the adventurer’s name, the date their profile was created, the three real-world level rewards and the agreement confirmation.
  • Preferences include the game mode, times table, timer, music, sound effects and visual settings.
  • Game history records completed rounds and Journey stages, when they were completed and how long they took. The app uses these records to show progress and fastest times.

Data collection and sharing

Children can play at home or at school without an online account. The developer does not collect players’ personal data through the app, sell their data or share game records with third parties.

The app has no ads, analytics, social features or messaging. It does not track people across apps or websites or collect data from location services, contacts, photos, the camera or the microphone.

Removing an adventurer

On the setup screen, tap Log Out. Tap the bin beside the adventurer’s name, type delete and tap Delete. This removes their profile, reward agreement, preferences and game history from GreenSquare on this device.

Website and support

GitHub Pages hosts this website. When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address for security. Read GitHub’s explanation of data collection on Pages.

If you contact David Ramsay, he uses the message and contact details you choose to provide to reply to your enquiry. For privacy questions, email David Ramsay or use the support contact details.
